Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy describes the information Dime Bot uses to provide its Discord features and how that information is handled.

Current availability

Dime Bot is currently in development and operated primarily for the Dime Discord server. If it is opened to other servers, this policy will be reviewed and updated.

Information the bot uses

To provide its features, Dime Bot may process Discord user, server, and channel IDs; usernames and configured nicknames; permission assignments; moderation settings and records; and bot settings such as custom messages.

When a command or gag feature is used, the bot processes relevant message or command content. Original gagged messages may be held temporarily in memory for the “view original message” feature. Dime Bot does not intentionally keep a general archive of server messages.

How information is used

Information is used to provide bot commands, check configured permissions, perform moderation features, maintain settings, and record management or error events.

Storage and sharing

Some settings and records are stored in files on the server hosting the bot. Discord processes interactions and messages under its own policies. The hosting provider may process or store information as needed to run the bot.

Dime Bot does not sell information or use it for advertising.

Retention and requests

Permission settings and other configuration are kept while needed to operate the bot. The management audit log is limited to recent entries. Temporary message data is kept only as needed for its feature and may be lost when the bot restarts.

To ask a question or request review or removal of bot-stored information, contact the bot owner using the details below.
